# Product Concept

## Customer journey
Discover (capsule) -> Own -> Bond -> Train -> Compete -> Collect

## Capsule
Sealed ball with a robotic creature. Rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary. Publish odds, avoid duplicate traps, offer exchange or guaranteed categories. No gambling-like paid random draws aimed at kids.

## Original creatures (safe, simulated abilities)
| Creature | Ability | Safe implementation | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | Electric charge | Glowing horns, crackle sound, game effect; no real shock | Pushing |
| Lizard | Grabbing tongue | Soft retractable silicone tongue; no adhesive | Capture |
| Dinosaur | Flame blast | LEDs, sound, optional safe mist; no real fire | Area control |
| Octopus | Tentacle grab | Soft low-force tentacles; sealed water spray only if leak-proof | Collection |

## Competitions (fun, not aggressive)
Robot soccer, obstacle challenge, treasure hunt, dance competition, team rescue. Equal specs within each class; no pay-to-win. Hybrid style: soft physical contact only, sensors/software decide scoring.

## Training
Behaviour-based for younger kids; visual block programming (Scratch-like) for older kids. Progression: experience, skills, cosmetics.

## Revenue ideas
Robot sales, capsules, parts/accessories/shells, events and tournaments, sponsorships, optional app subscription, competition videos (marketing first, ad revenue later).
