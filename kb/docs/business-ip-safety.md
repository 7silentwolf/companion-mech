# Business, IP, Safety

## IP
- Do not use Pokemon, Transformers or other third-party characters, names, logos or distinctive designs. Licensing for an independent inventor is uncertain and has no public pricing.
- You can own original character art/names/lore (copyright), brand names (trademark), eligible product appearance (design registration in India), software (copyright).
- Patents only for genuinely novel mechanisms or systems; not required to start. Do not publicly disclose a possible invention before talking to a patent professional.

## Child safety
Rounded soft shell, low-torque motors, no pinch points, protected batteries, emergency stop, no flames/projectiles, secured small parts.

## Compliance (verify)
- Earlier chats cited BIS toy certification: IS 9873 (mechanical/physical/flammability/chemical) and IS 15644 (electric toys), under the Toys Quality Control Order for toys for children under 14.
- Children's data: parental consent, no public chat, minimal data; check current Indian data-protection requirements.

## Risks
- Unit cost vs toy price (servos, battery, shell, shipping, certification).
- Phone dependency for young kids; parental controls needed.
- Hard mechanics: walking, soft tentacles, retractable tongue, water.
- Cloud AI and kids' voice data: prefer local/predefined behaviours first.
- Market proof: ask parents/kids to pay or put down a deposit before heavy building.
